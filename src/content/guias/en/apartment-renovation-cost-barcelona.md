If you are thinking about renovating your apartment, the first question is always the same: **how much is this going to cost me?** In this guide we give you real numbers — the same prices per square metre we work with at KOBOR — so you can form an honest idea before asking anyone for a quote.

## Price per square metre: the three finish levels

In a full renovation the price is calculated per m² and depends above all on the level of finishes. These are our indicative "from" prices:

| Finish level | Price | What it includes |
|---|---|---|
| **Basic** | from **€590/m²** | Functional materials and standard brands. A solid renovation, without frills. |
| **Standard** | from **€720/m²** | The value-for-money balance: recognised brands and better finishes. The most chosen option. |
| **Premium** | from **€890/m²** | High-end materials, made-to-measure joinery and design details. |

> **Important:** these are indicative "from" prices, VAT not included. The final amount depends on the floor area, the condition of the property, access and the materials chosen. That is why a serious price can only be given **after a visit** — which in our case is free.

## Examples by apartment size

Applying the standard finish level (€720/m²) to the most common sizes in Barcelona:

- **60 m² apartment** → from around €43,000
- **75 m² apartment** → from around €54,000
- **90 m² apartment** → from around €65,000

With the basic level, a 75 m² apartment would start at around €44,000; with premium, at around €67,000. You will find the complete rate list, line item by line item, on [our prices](/en/our-prices/).

## Example: an apartment of about 80 m²

An apartment of about 80 m² sits between the 75 m² and 90 m² examples above. The sum uses the same indicative “from” rates as this guide and [our prices](/en/our-prices/) (a full renovation from €590/m²). VAT is not included. This is not a closed quote.

| Finish level | Calculation | Estimate |
|---|---|---|
| **Basic** | 80 m² × €590/m² | from **€47,200** |
| **Standard** | 80 m² × €720/m² | from **€57,600** |
| **Premium** | 80 m² × €890/m² | from **€71,200** |

The figure is the exact product of the “from” rate and 80 m². On a real job the amount changes with the condition of the apartment, access and the materials. The quote we sign only comes after the free visit.

## What does a full renovation include?

When we at KOBOR talk about a [full home renovation](/en/home-renovation-barcelona/), we mean **one single team for the entire project**, from start to finish:

- Kitchens: units, worktop, appliances and installation
- Bathrooms: plumbing, tiling, sanitaryware and shower screens
- Layout: partition walls, demolition and new rooms
- Renewed electrical and plumbing installations
- Flooring, painting and interior joinery
- Heating and cooling, insulation and ventilation

A single person responsible from start to finish means: no miscoordination between trades, no "that is not my job", and a quote **closed before work begins** — no surprises halfway through.
