The bathroom is the most requested renovation in the home: it is small, it is used every day and it ages quickly. The good news is that it is also the renovation with the clearest price. At KOBOR, a **complete bathroom renovation starts at €4,900** (VAT not included). Here is exactly what that price includes and what can make it vary.

## What a complete bathroom renovation includes

- Renewed **plumbing** (supply and waste)
- **Tiling** of walls and floor
- **Sanitaryware**: toilet, basin and taps
- Shower **screen**
- Waste removal and final clean

In other words: you start with a 1990s bathroom and end up with a finished bathroom, ready to use — with a single team doing everything, so you are not coordinating a plumber, a tiler and a fitter separately.

## What can push the price up (or down)

| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| Replacing a bathtub with a shower tray | The most common change; usually within the range |
| Moving sanitaryware to a new position | Increases the price: it means moving waste pipes and supply points |
| Quality of tiles and taps | The gap between basic and premium ranges is wide |
| Condition of the original installation | Surprises in old plumbing = extra line items; that is why we visit before quoting |
| Size of the bathroom | The "from" prices refer to standard apartment bathrooms |

Every one of those line items is published with its price on [our prices](/en/our-prices/), so you can form an idea before you even call us. Even so, the honest way to give you an exact figure is to see the space: that is why the **visit and the quote are free**, and the quote is **closed** — line items, materials and timings in writing before we start. [Request it from our contact page](/en/contact/) whenever it suits you.

## How long does bathroom work take?

A complete standard bathroom is usually a short job compared with a full renovation. We give you the exact timeline in writing in the quote, alongside the line items — so from day 1 you know when you get your bathroom back.
